# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-22)
- Mark mode: colour keys in four named colours to map out your voice (shortcut M); Fit to marks (N); legend; marks in the overview strip; saved on the device.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-22)
- First version, split out of Part Coach's full-screen piano: full 88 keys, overview strip and zoom, sustain pedal, octave moves, key labels, chord mode, loop pedal with layers.
- New: playing from the laptop keyboard (by key position, works on QWERTZ), keyboard shortcuts with a ? list, Keys only mode, Full screen button, volume, screen stays awake, settings remembered, installable as its own app.
- Left out on purpose: interval practice and everything song-related.
